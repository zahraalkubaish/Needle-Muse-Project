About Needle & Muse

Our Mission

Needle & Muse is a creative community uniting Club Members, Activity Leaders and Instructors, and Club Managers through a shared passion for fashion, creativity, styling, and self-expression. Fashion is more than clothing — it is a language, a craft, and a form of art that everyone deserves to explore.

We offer hands-on workshops, sustainability programs, and practical training in sewing, sketching, and design. Club Members—university students from complete beginners to experienced creatives—explore offerings, participate in studio workshops, develop their skills, and evaluate completed activities.

Activity Leaders and Instructors share their expertise, lead workshops, and mentor students across our creative learning experiences, while Club Managers organize offerings, coordinate instructors, and manage club activities so our community flourishes.
