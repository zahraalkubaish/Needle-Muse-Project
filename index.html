Where Fashion Begins!

Needle & Muse is a university fashion club platform built for three dedicated audiences: Club Members, Activity Leaders and Instructors, and Club Managers who share a passion for fashion, creativity, and self-expression.

Club Members—university students ranging from beginners curious about sewing to aspiring designers—explore club offerings, participate in hands-on styling, sketching, and garment workshops, develop practical skills, and evaluate their learning experiences.

Activity Leaders and Instructors share their expertise, lead studio workshops, and contribute to the club's creative learning experiences, while Club Managers organize offerings, coordinate instructors, and manage club activities.
